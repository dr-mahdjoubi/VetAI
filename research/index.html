VetAI Global

Research by Abdenasser Mahdjoubi, DVM — artificial intelligence in veterinary diagnostics, readiness assessment, and animal bioacoustics.

ORCID: https://orcid.org/0009-0007-9528-7049

Important: works listed below are preprints, not peer-reviewed journal articles, unless stated otherwise. VTRI is a proposed, unvalidated score. AI classifies patterns in animal sounds and images; it does not translate animal language. AI supports. The vet decides.

1. Artificial Intelligence in Veterinary Medicine: From Laboratory Accuracy to Clinical Readiness

Secondary analysis of 39 deep-learning diagnostic studies, a real-world benefit–harm appraisal, and the Veterinary Translational Readiness Index (VTRI).

Key findings: 82.1% of studies addressed dogs or cats, 10.3% livestock or horses; median sample size 837.5; VTRI scores ranged from 2 to 7 of 15.

Preprint: Zenodo, 16 October 2026, v1 — doi.org/10.5281/ZENODO.23423653
Also on SSRN: doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7621186
